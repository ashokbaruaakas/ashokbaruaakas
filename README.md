## What I've built

- **Payments at a licensed e-money institution.** Core payments engineer on [SiPay](https://sipay.com.tr/en/) (Türkiye): merchant and mobile banking, link / P2P / B2B / QR / bill-pay / POS flows, and HSM-backed saved cards. Led a team of six engineers for my final four months (sprint planning, code reviews, hiring), built the platform side of the HSM integration for PCI-DSS-aligned card storage, drove core parts of the PHP 7.4 to 8.2 upgrade, and built microservices instrumented with Elasticsearch and Graylog.
- **A whole business on one platform.** At [Grow More Gaze](https://growmoregaze.com) I own all the technology and run the operations platform for an international agency: ordering, invoicing, HRM, payroll, email campaigns, and **seven payment gateways** with idempotent webhook handling. I also own the servers, security and CI/CD with zero-downtime deploys and monitoring, and work agent-first: every change that touches money, data or auth gets my review.
- **Software that talks to hardware.** HRM and payroll for a multi-site workforce at Stellar BD, with fingerprint attendance devices synced in real time over MQTT.
- **Client work.** Independent contractor since May 2026: built and maintain [bizztechsz.com](https://bizztechsz.com), a bulk-order quote and company portfolio platform.
- **Open source.** I build open-source Laravel tooling under [**@thesoftpulze**](https://github.com/thesoftpulze) (LaraVibe Standards and [LaraVibe-Vue](https://github.com/thesoftpulze/laravibe-vue)), and maintain [clawkit](https://github.com/ashokbaruaakas/clawkit) (700+ GHCR pulls), Notify Fanout and Proton Pass Secret Fetcher. Merged contributions to [Inertia.js](https://github.com/inertiajs/inertia-laravel) and [Raycast Extensions](https://github.com/raycast/extensions). Full timeline: [ashokbaruaakas.com/open-source](https://ashokbaruaakas.com/open-source).
